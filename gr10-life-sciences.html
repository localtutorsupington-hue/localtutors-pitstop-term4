Pit Stop: Term 4
In person in Upington

Grade 10 Life Sciences in English

28 to 30 September, in person in Upington.

This bootcamp is only for Grade 10 learners whose language of instruction is English.

Who it's for

For Grade 10 learners at Afrikaans medium schools whose language of instruction is English, who need Life Sciences taught properly, in English, before Term 4 starts.

The problem

English learners in Afrikaans majority schools often get the shorter, rushed version of the lesson, then have to write the exam in English. That gap adds up over a year.

How the days work

  • 1
    Day 1: we find the gaps from Term 2 and Term 3.
  • 2
    Day 2: we close those gaps, and your child explains every concept back in English.
  • 3
    Day 3: exam questions, and working ahead into Term 4.

What's included

Taught fully in English, in a small English only group

3 weeks of lessons after the camp, completely free
