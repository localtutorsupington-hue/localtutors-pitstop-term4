PIT STOP: TERM 4

3-Day Pit Stop Bootcamp: Grade 10 Life Sciences in English

A 3-day holiday bootcamp only for Grade 10 learners who are taught Life Sciences in English. In a small English-only group, Rolien Britz finds the gaps from Term 2 and 3, closes them, and works ahead into Term 4. Nothing gets translated, nothing gets rushed.

3 days English only 4 spots left In person, Upington
This bootcamp is only for Grade 10 learners whose language of instruction is English.

Who it's for

This is for your Grade 10 if:

  • their school teaches Life Sciences mainly in Afrikaans, but they write the exam in English
  • they say they follow the lesson, but battle to explain the concepts back in English
  • you're not sure they're getting the full version of every lesson, not just the shortened one
  • you want them taught by someone who already works with English medium learners every week

The problem

English learners in Afrikaans majority schools often get the shorter, rushed version of the lesson, then have to write the exam in English. That gap adds up over a year.

This Pit Stop closes that gap before Term 4 starts, not after the exam.

How the days work

  • 1
    Day 1: we find the gaps from Term 2 and Term 3.
  • 2
    Day 2: we close those gaps, and your child explains every concept back in English.
  • 3
    Day 3: exam questions, and working ahead into Term 4.

What's included

Taught fully in English, in a small English only group

3 weeks of lessons after the camp, completely free
